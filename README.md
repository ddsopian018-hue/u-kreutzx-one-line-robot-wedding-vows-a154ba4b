One line of wedding vows for a robot couple: "I take you to be my wife, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, till death or a forced update do us part." It should win because it is the classic vow itself, not a gag bolted onto one — the promise is real, the rhythm is the rhythm every guest in the room already knows, and the machine swaps in at exactly the two load-bearing words: "health" becomes "low battery", and "till death do us part" is interrupted by "or a forced update". The laugh lands in the last few words, needs no explanation, and could not work for a human couple.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` (one line, 120 characters) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` when unset).
